MR C Uploads

A personal tool for publishing my own music to my own YouTube channels.

MR C Uploads is a small private program I wrote for myself. I write and release music, and publishing each track by hand means typing the same title, description, tags and settings into YouTube over and over. This tool does that part for me.

What it does

  • Uploads finished music videos to YouTube channels that I own.
  • Fills in the title, description, tags, category and language from files I have already written and checked.
  • Attaches the thumbnail and the subtitle file that belong to that video.
  • Schedules a video to go public at a chosen date and time.
  • Creates playlists on my channels and adds my own videos to them.
  • Reads back how many of my videos are live, scheduled or still drafts, so I can see where a release stands.

Who can use it

Nobody but me. It is not a product, there is no sign-up, and it is not offered to anyone else. It runs on my own computer, signs in as me, and only ever touches channels I own. It is registered with Google purely because that is the only way an ordinary program is allowed to talk to YouTube on its owner's behalf.

Privacy

The tool stores nothing about anyone and sends nothing to anyone. What it does with the Google permissions it asks for is set out in full here: Privacy Policy.
