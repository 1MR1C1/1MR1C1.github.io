MR C Uploads is a personal program written and used by one person — its author — to publish his own music to YouTube channels he owns. These terms describe what it is and what may be expected of it.

Who may use it

The author, and nobody else. It is not a product, not a service, and not offered for sale, licence or public use. There is no sign-up and no account to create. It runs on the author's own computer and acts only on channels he owns.

What it does

It uploads the author's own finished videos to his own YouTube channels, fills in the title, description, tags, category, language and publish time from files he has already written, attaches his own thumbnails and subtitle files, creates playlists on his channels, and reads back his own list of videos so he can see which are live, scheduled or still drafts. It does nothing else.

Acceptable use

  • Only the author's own work is uploaded through it.
  • It is not used to access, alter or collect anything belonging to anyone else.
  • It is used in line with YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's API policies.

The author may stop using the tool, or withdraw its access to Google at any time, from Google Account permissions. Doing so immediately ends everything the tool is able to do.
